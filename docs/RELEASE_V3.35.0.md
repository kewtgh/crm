# Lumina CRM v3.35.0

## Workspaces

- Unified semantic icons for all authenticated navigation destinations and operational page headings.
- Consistent workspace accents, section icons, selected tabs and summary surfaces across work, relationships, student services, commercial, management, governance and settings areas.
- Distinct text/status labels remain authoritative; color is supplementary. Authentication and public portal branding remain separate.
- Refined Contact commercial information, Lead actions, Organization products, agreements, family forms and Revenue configuration guidance.
- Student support includes monthly activity trends, health distribution and compact product/cohort comparisons.

## Purchasers and finance

- Personal purchasers and institutional purchasers are separate. An institutional representative is recorded independently and must belong to that institution.
- Personal purchasing follows quote creation, acceptance and contract conversion; the existing direct Contract draft form remains organization/household based.
- Student selectors accept canonical Student records. Purchasing and enrollment filters are independent; commercial product filters include contracts without student enrollment.
- Settlement charts preserve currencies and distinguish receivable schedules and current settlement from recognized Revenue.
- Quote creation retains exact request identity after uncertain responses; accepted writes recover through refresh only.

## Upgrade and boundaries

Version 3.35.0 includes migration 126 (131 SQL files). Prior migrations are unchanged.
No automatic buyer reclassification or financial backfill occurs. Apply the normal migration process before using the updated application.
Revenue recognition, correction, cash-application and Commission accounting semantics are unchanged.
Historical research files remain local under exact ignore rules; public checkouts do not require them.
This checkpoint does not deploy the application or access Production.
